### Module Behavior and Property Meaning
This module counts how many times a signed input j satisfies 1 <= j < 200, and adds each such j to a sum i, as long as i < 200.

- i: accumulates eligible j values.
- k: counts how many such values were processed.

At each positive clock edge:
- If i < 200 and 1 <= j < 200, then:
    - i is incremented by j
    - k is incremented by 1

This continues as long as i is less than 200.
The property asserts that k never exceeds 200. Since each increment of k is tied to adding a j >= 1 to i, and i stops accumulating once it reaches 200, k must remain ≤ 200.

### Proposed Lemmas:
We propose a lemma which strengthens the property by adding the invariant k <= i.
Every time k is incremented, at least 1 is added to i, so i grows at least as fast as k.

The property alone (k <= 200) is not 1-inductive. The lemma is 1-inductive, and hence might be easier to prove directly.
It connects k and i, establishing that k <= i, and since i < 200 is the guard for continuing the process, this implies that k must also remain below 200.
