This SystemVerilog module defines a simple sequential circuit where three 32-bit registers a, b, and c rotate their values on every rising clock edge.
**Module Behavior:**

- Initial values:
  - `a = 1`, `b = 2`, `c = 3`
- On each `posedge clk`, the values are rotated:
  - `a <= b`
  - `b <= c`
  - `c <= a`
- This forms a cyclic permutation of the initial values (1, 2, 3):
  - After 1 cycle: a=2, b=3, c=1
  - After 2 cycles: a=3, b=1, c=2
  - After 3 cycles: a=1, b=2, c=3 (back to start)
So over time, the values (1,2,3) rotate among the registers.

**Property Meaning:**
The property is a safety property which asserts that on every clock cycle, a and b must hold different values.
Given the rotation of distinct values among a, b, and c, this condition will always be true, assuming distinct initial values.

**Proposed Lemmas:**
To support the verification of the property over time, we propose a lemma that constitutes a stronger invariant capturing the mutual distinctness of all three registers. 
This invariant holds in the initial state and is preserved under the cyclic updates, making it 1-inductive and easier to verify. 
Since it implies the original property, it effectively aids the verification process.
