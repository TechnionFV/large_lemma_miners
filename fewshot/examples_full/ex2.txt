
### Module Behavior and Property Meaning:
This module defines four 8-bit registers: x, y, z, and k, all initialized to 0.

On every rising edge of the clock:
- If x < 64:
  - If k % 3 == 0, then x is incremented by 1.
  - y and z are incremented by 1.
  - k is updated to x + y + z.

As a result:
- y and z increase on every clock cycle.
- x increases only on cycles when k is divisible by 3.


The property asserts that on every clock cycle, x, y, and z are all equal. 
It is nontrivial since x is guarded by an additional condition (k % 3 == 0) while y and z are incremented unconditionally.

### Proposed Lemmas:

We propose a lemma that strengthens the property by adding the conjunct that k is divisible by 3.
This conjunct means that, despite the differing static update conditions, all three variables are in fact incremented on every cycle.
The lemma is 1-inductive: it holds in the initial state, and if `x == y == z` and `k % 3 == 0` at some cycle, then all three variables are incremented by 1 in the next cycle. As a result, their equality is preserved, and the updated value of k becomes three times the new value of x, which remains divisible by 3.
